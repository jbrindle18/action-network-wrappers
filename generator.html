Add to Action Network

Four steps, about two minutes. Keep Action Network open in another tab.

1

Create a new wrapper in Action Network

Details → Page Wrappers → Add New Wrapper

2

Copy the header

Paste it into the Header box.

3

Copy the footer

Paste it into the Footer box.

4

Save, preview, and switch your action to it

Check it in AN’s Preview, then choose this wrapper in your action’s settings.

Using custom thank-you pages? Turn off Action Network’s own share buttons on the action, or people will see two sets.
Save a copy of your settings so you can come back and edit them later.
